Every bird you’ve seen, on your own map.

Wingmark is an iPhone app for keeping a record of the birds you spot: what it was, where, when, and a photo if you got one.

Log a sighting in seconds

Take or pick a photo and Wingmark fills in the place and time from it. Add the species if you know it, or save it as unidentified and come back later.

Look birds up

The built-in guide has photos, descriptions, habitat and diet for each species, plus recordings of their calls and songs.

Earn badges

Badges mark milestones as your list of sightings and species grows.

Keep it to yourself

Your sightings are visible only to you. There are no ads and no tracking. Wingmark is available in English and Turkish.

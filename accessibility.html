Accessibility

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page lists the iPhone accessibility features Wingmark supports, based on what we have checked, and the ones we have not verified yet.

What we support

  • Dark Interface. All screens work in dark mode.
  • Larger Text. The app works at the largest text size. We found places where text overlapped and fixed them.
  • Differentiate Without Color Alone. We don’t rely on colour alone. Badges show text and an icon. Errors and password rules have icons. Map pins change size and show a ring when selected. Life stage and sex are text chips, and active filter chips show a checkmark.
  • Reduced Motion. The pin, form pin and map animations turn off when Reduce Motion is on.

What we don’t claim yet

  • VoiceOver. We have not verified Wingmark with VoiceOver yet.
  • Voice Control. We have not verified Wingmark with Voice Control yet.
  • Captions and audio descriptions. Not applicable. The app has no video, and the bird sounds are recordings without speech.

Tell us about a problem

If something in Wingmark is hard to use, email us at support.wingmark@gmail.com. Tell us what happened, which accessibility setting you use, and your iPhone model and iOS version.
